How to Import Cleaning Supplies from China to Saudi Arabia & GCC (2026 Guide)

Last updated: November 2026 · 7 min read

GCC governments are investing heavily in tourism, hospitality, and mega-projects (NEOM, Red Sea Project, Expo City). This drives massive demand for cleaning supplies — detergents, disinfectants, janitorial tools, and paper products.

China is the world's largest manufacturer of cleaning chemicals and tools, offering 30–50% cost savings over European brands. Here's how to import compliantly and profitably.

1. Product Categories in Demand

Liquid Detergents

Laundry, dishwash, hand soap, floor cleaner, glass cleaner. 1L–5L bottles. Custom formulation available.

Disinfectants & Sanitizers

Surface disinfectants, hand sanitizers (alcohol-based & alcohol-free). 500ml–20L. Hospital-grade options.

Janitorial Tools

Mops, buckets, brushes, squeegees, microfiber cloths, wringer trolleys. Bulk packaging.

Paper Products

Toilet rolls, kitchen towels, facial tissues, napkins. Virgin & recycled options. Jumbo rolls for dispensers.

2. Compliance & Documentation

Cleaning chemicals face strict regulation in the GCC. Required documents:

  • MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheet): Mandatory for all chemical products. Must be in English (Arabic translation recommended).
  • SASO / SABER: Required for detergents and disinfectants entering Saudi Arabia.
  • ESMA: Required for UAE imports.
  • ISO 9001 / 14001: Factory certification (not mandatory but preferred by large buyers).
  • COA (Certificate of Analysis): Batch-specific test results for chemical composition.

⚠️ Never ship chemicals without MSDS — airlines and shipping lines will reject them, and customs will seize undeclared hazardous materials.

4. Private Label Opportunities

Retailers and distributors across the GCC are looking for private-label cleaning brands to compete with imported European names. Chinese manufacturers offer:

  • Custom formulation (scent, concentration, pH level)
  • Custom bottle design and label (English + Arabic)
  • Bulk packaging for hospitality (5L–20L refill sizes)
  • Small-batch private label at 1,000-unit MOQ

5. Shipping Considerations for Chemicals

Liquid detergents and disinfectants are classified as **non-hazardous** in most cases (check MSDS). They ship as general cargo by sea. However:

  • Alcohol-based sanitizers (>70% alcohol) may be classified as hazardous — confirm with your freight forwarder
  • Some shipping lines require DG (Dangerous Goods) declaration even for low-concentration products
  • Pack in HDPE bottles with tamper-evident caps to prevent leaks
