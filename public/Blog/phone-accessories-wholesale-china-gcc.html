Wholesale Phone Accessories from China to GCC: Complete Import Guide (2026)

Last updated: November 2026 · 8 min read

The GCC phone accessories market is one of the fastest-moving B2B segments — high turnover, strong margins, and constant demand. But importing profitably from China requires navigating certifications, MOQs, and quality control.

This guide covers everything GCC importers need to know in 2026.

1. Top Products to Import in 2026

Phone Cases

TPU, silicone, leather, magnetic wallet cases. For iPhone 15/16, Samsung S24/S25. Custom logo & retail packaging.

Chargers & Adapters

20W–65W GaN chargers, USB-C multiports. CE/FCC certified. Retail-ready blister packs.

TWS Earbuds

ANC, ENC, Bluetooth 5.3. White-label options. Competitive with AirPods in feature set at 1/10th the price.

Cables & Power Banks

MFi-compatible cables, 10000mAh–30000mAh power banks. Braided cables for durability.

4. Private Labeling & Packaging

Most factories offer OEM services:

  • Custom logo printing on cases, chargers, earbud cases
  • Retail packaging design (blister, box, pouch)
  • Multi-language packaging (English + Arabic for GCC market)
  • Barcode / SKU labeling for retail distribution

Typical setup cost: $100–$500 depending on complexity. Packaging MOQ is usually 1,000 units.

5. Shipping to GCC

Most phone accessories ship by sea to Jebel Ali (Dubai) or Dammam (KSA). A 20ft container fits ~200,000–300,000 small items (cases, cables). For smaller orders, LCL (Less than Container Load) or air freight for urgent stock.
