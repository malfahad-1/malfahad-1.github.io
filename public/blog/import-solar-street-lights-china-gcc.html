How to Import Solar & Commercial LED Street Lights from China to GCC (2026 Guide)

Last updated: November 2026 · 7 min read

Outdoor and industrial LED lighting — including street lights, floodlights, and warehouse high bays — is one of the highest-margin import categories across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, and Qatar. However, extreme Gulf summer temperatures (50°C+), fine desert dust, and coastal humidity quickly destroy budget fixtures built with cheap components.

1. Essential Specs for GCC Climate Conditions

Component Recommended GCC Spec Why It Matters
LED Chips & Efficacy 140–170 lm/W (Lumileds, Osram, or high-bin SMD3030/5050) Meets SASO/ESMA energy-efficiency tiers with lower heat output
LED Driver (AC Models) MEAN WELL, Inventronics, or Philips with 6kV–10kV surge protection Prevents early driver burnout during voltage fluctuations
Solar Battery (Solar Models) Grade-A LiFePO4 (Lithium Iron Phosphate), 3–5 rainy-day autonomy Tolerates high ambient heat safely without swelling (avoid ternary lithium)
Housing & Protection ADC12 die-cast aluminum, IP66 weatherproof, IK08–IK10 impact rated Blocks fine sand ingress and resists corrosion in coastal cities

2. Typical MOQs & Lead Times

  • AC LED Street Lights (50W–200W): MOQ 100–300 pcs; 15–20 days production.
  • All-in-One Solar Street Lights (60W–150W): MOQ 100–200 sets; 18–25 days production (includes UN38.3 battery testing documentation).
  • IP66 Floodlights & High Bays (100W–400W): MOQ 300–500 pcs; 12–18 days production.

3. Compliance & Dangerous Goods Shipping Notes

AC street lights and floodlights require SABER (PCoC + SCoC) and SASO energy efficiency registration for Saudi Arabia, or ECAS for the UAE. For integrated solar street lights containing LiFePO4 batteries, ensure your factory provides a valid UN38.3 report and MSDS so your freight forwarder can book them cleanly as Class 9 Dangerous Goods.
