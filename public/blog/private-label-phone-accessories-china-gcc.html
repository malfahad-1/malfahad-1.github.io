Private Label Phone Accessories from China: OEM Packaging & Compliance Guide (2026)

Last updated: November 2026 · 7 min read

Selling generic unbranded cables and chargers in the GCC puts you in a race to the bottom on price. Launching your own private-label (OEM) brand allows retailers and distributors in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Kuwait to command 35–60% retail margins and build repeat customer loyalty.

1. OEM Customization Options & Typical MOQs

Customization Level What You Get Typical MOQ
Light OEM (Logo + Box) Laser-engraved or silkscreened logo on existing factory housing + custom-printed retail box 500–1,000 pcs per SKU
Packaging Consolidation Unified brand color palette, hang-tabs, and inner trays across cables, chargers & cases 1,000 retail boxes per size
Custom Color / Finish Pantone-matched cable braiding, matte silicone coatings, or metallic charger trims 2,000–3,000 pcs

2. GCC Retail Packaging Checklist

Before approving your factory's die-line artwork, make sure your retail box includes:

  • Bilingual Arabic + English text: Product name, input/output electrical specs (e.g., Input: 100–240V~ 50/60Hz), and safety warnings.
  • Permanent "Made in China" origin mark: Printed clearly on both the product body and retail packaging.
  • EAN-13 / UPC Barcode: Required for SABER registration in Saudi Arabia and retail POS scanning across the GCC.
  • GCC-compatible plug specs: Specify UK/GCC 3-pin (Type G) wall plugs on GaN chargers rather than US 2-pin plugs.

3. Using Existing Factory Certifications Under Your Brand

Testing a brand-new charger or TWS earbud from scratch for IEC/CB and SASO IECEE takes weeks and thousands of dollars. Smart importers select proven factory models that already hold valid IEC/CB test reports and sign a factory OEM declaration linking your brand name to the existing test report.
