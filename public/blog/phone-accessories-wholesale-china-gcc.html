Wholesale Phone Accessories from China to GCC: Complete Import Guide (2026)

Last updated: November 2026 · 8 min read

The GCC phone accessories market is one of the fastest-moving B2B segments — high turnover, strong margins, and constant demand. But importing profitably from China requires navigating certifications, MOQs, and quality control.

This guide covers everything GCC importers need to know in 2026.

1. Top Products to Import in 2026

Phone Cases

TPU, silicone, leather, and magnetic wallet cases for current iPhone and Samsung Galaxy series. Custom logo & retail packaging.

Chargers & Adapters

20W–65W GaN chargers and USB-C PD multiport adapters with UK/GCC 3-pin plugs. CE/RoHS certified in retail-ready packs.

TWS Earbuds

ANC, ENC noise reduction, and Bluetooth 5.3 models with white-label branding and custom retail boxes at strong wholesale margins.

Cables & Power Banks

High-durability braided USB-C/Lightning cables and 10,000mAh–30,000mAh USB-C power banks.

2. Certification Requirements

Market Requirements
Saudi Arabia SABER (PCoC + SCoC) & SASO IECEE Recognition for chargers, power banks, and wireless earbuds; USB-C charging port mandate applies
UAE MoIAT / ECAS for electrical chargers & adapters; TDRA type approval for Bluetooth/wireless earbuds
Qatar QCD / QS conformity for regulated electronics
All Markets CE, RoHS, and valid IEC/CB test reports (issued within 3 years) from the factory

⚠️ Power banks and wall chargers face the strictest customs scrutiny in Saudi Arabia and the UAE. Budget 3–4 weeks for SASO IECEE / SABER or ECAS registration before shipping.

4. Private Labeling & Packaging

Most Shenzhen and Dongguan factories offer full OEM/private-label services:

  • Custom laser or silkscreen logo printing on cases, chargers, and earbud charging cases
  • Retail packaging design (window box, rigid magnetic box, blister pack, or hang-pouch)
  • Bilingual packaging (English + Arabic specifications, importer details, and ratings for GCC retail)
  • Barcode / EAN / SKU labeling ready for hypermarket and e-commerce fulfillment

Typical plate/mold setup cost: $100–$500 depending on packaging finish. Custom retail box MOQ is usually 1,000 units.

5. Shipping & Battery Rules for GCC

Standard accessories (cases, cables, wall chargers) ship as general cargo by sea to Jebel Ali (Dubai) or Dammam (KSA) — a 20ft container fits roughly 200,000–300,000 small retail-packed items, while smaller orders move via LCL or air freight.

For power banks and lithium-battery audio products, ensure your factory provides a current UN38.3 test report and MSDS: sea carriers book them as Class 9 Dangerous Goods, and air shipments require cargo-aircraft booking with batteries at ≤30% state of charge.
