Pre-Shipment Quality Inspection in China: Checklist for GCC Importers (2026)

Last updated: November 2026 · 7 min read

Once a shipment leaves Shenzhen, Ningbo, or Guangzhou for Jebel Ali, Dammam, or Hamad Port, returning defective goods is virtually impossible. That is why every experienced GCC importer enforces a strict Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI) before releasing the 70% balance payment.

Use this practical quality control checklist to verify quantity, workmanship, product safety, and export packaging before your goods are loaded.

1. When Should Inspection Happen?

Schedule your pre-shipment inspection when 100% of the goods are manufactured and at least 80% are packed into export cartons. Inspecting too early leaves uninspected units at the end of the production run; inspecting after container loading is too late to fix defects.

2. Category-Specific QC Checks

Category Key On-Site Tests Critical Failure Points
LED Lighting Wattage & lumen check, 2–4 hour aging/burn-in test, color temperature (CCT) consistency, driver labeling Under-wattage drivers, flickering, wrong base (E27 vs B22), missing SASO/ESMA markings
Phone Accessories Fast-charge PD wattage test, UK/GCC 3-pin plug fit, Bluetooth pairing & mic check, battery capacity check Loose USB-C ports, fake GaN wattage, missing UN38.3/MSDS on power banks
Cleaning Supplies Handle torque & pole thickness, bristle retention, bottle leak/drop test, net fill weight & pH check Cracked buckets, leaking caps in transit, missing Arabic/English warning labels

3. The 6-Point Export Packaging & Labeling Audit

  • Carton count & dimensions: Verify total master cartons match the commercial packing list and container CBM calculation.
  • Drop test: Perform a standard carton drop test on retail-boxed electronics and lighting to ensure inner dividers prevent breakage.
  • Barcode & SKU scan: Scan unit barcodes to confirm they match your warehouse or hypermarket system.
  • GCC voltage & plug check: Confirm 220–240V / 50–60Hz ratings and UK 3-pin plugs (Type G) where required.
  • Country of origin: Ensure permanent "Made in China" marking is printed on both the product and outer packaging — customs in Saudi Arabia and the UAE reject unmarked goods.
  • Shipping marks: Confirm gross/net weight, carton numbering (e.g., 1/250), and consignee details on outer cartons.

4. Understanding AQL Sampling

Inspectors follow international AQL (Acceptable Quality Limit) standards (ISO 2859-1) rather than opening every single box. For most GCC wholesale orders:

  • Critical defects (safety hazards, wrong plug, missing origin mark): 0% tolerance
  • Major defects (non-working LED, dead charger, cracked housing): AQL 2.5
  • Minor defects (slight cosmetic scratch, minor box scuff): AQL 4.0
