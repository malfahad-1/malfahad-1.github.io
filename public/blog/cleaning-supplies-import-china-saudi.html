How to Import Cleaning Supplies from China to Saudi Arabia & GCC (2026 Guide)

Last updated: November 2026 · 7 min read

GCC governments are investing heavily in tourism, hospitality, and mega-projects (NEOM, Red Sea Project, Expo City). This drives massive demand for cleaning supplies — detergents, disinfectants, janitorial tools, and paper products.

China is the world's largest manufacturer of cleaning chemicals and tools, offering 30–50% cost savings over European brands. Here's how to import compliantly and profitably.

1. Product Categories in Demand

Liquid Detergents

Laundry, dishwash, hand soap, floor cleaner, and glass cleaner in 1L–5L bottles. Custom formulation available.

Disinfectants & Sanitizers

Surface disinfectants and hand sanitizers (alcohol-based & alcohol-free) in 500ml–20L packs. Hospital-grade options.

Janitorial Tools

Mops, buckets, brushes, squeegees, microfiber cloths, and wringer trolleys in bulk packaging.

Paper Products

Toilet rolls, kitchen towels, facial tissues, and napkins in virgin & recycled options, including jumbo dispenser rolls.

2. Compliance & Documentation

Cleaning chemicals face strict regulation in the GCC. Required documents include:

  • MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheet): Mandatory for all chemical products. Must be in English (Arabic translation recommended).
  • SASO / SABER: Required for detergents and disinfectants entering Saudi Arabia.
  • ESMA: Required for UAE imports.
  • ISO 9001 / 14001: Factory certification (not mandatory, but strongly preferred by institutional and hospitality buyers).
  • COA (Certificate of Analysis): Batch-specific test results verifying chemical composition.

⚠️ Never ship chemicals without an MSDS — airlines and shipping lines will reject them, and customs will seize undeclared hazardous materials.

4. Private Label Opportunities

Retailers and distributors across the GCC are launching private-label cleaning brands to compete with imported European names. Chinese manufacturers offer:

  • Custom formulation (scent, active concentration, pH level)
  • Custom bottle molds and bilingual labels (English + Arabic)
  • Bulk refill packaging for hospitality (5L–20L jerrycans)
  • Small-batch private label runs starting at 1,000-unit MOQ

5. Shipping Considerations for Chemicals

Liquid detergents and disinfectants are classified as non-hazardous in most cases (always verify via the factory MSDS) and ship as general cargo by sea. However:

  • Alcohol-based sanitizers (>70% alcohol) may be classified as flammable hazardous goods — confirm classification with your freight forwarder before booking
  • Some ocean carriers require a DG (Dangerous Goods) declaration even for low-concentration surfactants
  • Specify thick-walled HDPE bottles with induction seals and tamper-evident caps to prevent transit leaks
