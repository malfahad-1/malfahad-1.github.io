How to Verify a Chinese Factory Before Paying a Deposit (2026 Checklist)

Last updated: November 2026 · 6 min read

Thousands of listings on B2B marketplaces claim to be "direct manufacturers," yet many are middlemen operating out of small offices who subcontract your order to unknown workshops. Before you wire a 30% deposit, verifying who actually controls the production line protects both your pricing and your product quality.

1. Check the Chinese Business License & Business Scope

Every registered Chinese company holds a business license with an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Look closely at two fields:

  • Business Scope (经营范围): Real manufacturers include terms like "manufacturing" (制造) or "processing" (加工). If the scope only lists "sales" (销售) or "import/export" (进出口), the entity is a trading company.
  • Registered Address: Check whether the address is in an industrial zone (e.g., Guzhen/Zhongshan for LED lighting, Bao'an/Longhua in Shenzhen for phone accessories, or Yongkang/Ningbo for cleaning hardware) rather than a downtown office tower.

2. Verify Authenticity of Test Reports & Certifications

Do not accept cropped PDF screenshots of CE, RoHS, IEC/CB, or ISO certificates. Always confirm:

  • The company name on the test report matches the supplier's Chinese business license (or is linked via an official OEM authorization letter).
  • The model number on the report matches the exact SKU you are buying.
  • The certificate number is verifiable on the issuing laboratory's database (SGS, TÜV, Intertek, BV) and issued within the last 3 years for SABER/ECAS use.

3. Conduct a Live Video Walk-Through or On-Site Audit

A 10-minute unscripted WeChat video call from the factory floor reveals more than a polished PDF catalog. Ask to see:

  1. The factory gate sign showing the registered Chinese company name
  2. Raw material warehouse and SMT/assembly/injection molding lines
  3. Aging & testing benches (especially LED burn-in racks or charger load testers)
  4. Finished goods packing area with export cartons

4. Match the Bank Beneficiary Name to the License

⚠️ Never wire a deposit to a personal bank account or an unrelated third-party company name without a stamped authorization agreement. The beneficiary name on your Proforma Invoice (PI) should match the verified company's registered English name.
