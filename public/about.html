Our Story

Alfaha

Alfaha is a Wuhan-based sourcing agent helping GCC businesses import LED lighting, phone accessories, and cleaning tools directly from verified Chinese factories. We work on a simple commission-based model: you tell us what you need, we quote within 24–48 hours, and you pay the factory against clear milestones. No inventory risk, no fake suppliers.

We are based on the ground in Wuhan, China 🇨🇳 — close to the factories, speaking the language, and available in Gulf business hours. Our focus is the GCC: UAE · Saudi Arabia · Qatar · Oman · Kuwait.
